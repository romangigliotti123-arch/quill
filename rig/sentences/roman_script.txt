# Spoken-corpus script for the quill gauntlet.
#
# Read one line at a time, exactly as written. Blank lines and #-comments are
# ignored. THE LINE IS THE GROUND TRUTH — if you improvise, the word error rate
# for that clip measures your improvisation, not the app. If you fluff one, redo
# it: rig/record_voice.sh --redo 12
#
# Order is deliberate. If you stop half way, the half that got recorded is still
# the half worth having.
#
#   1-10   Short. Under two seconds each, and they are the risk case: the
#          recogniser has a floor on how fast it can say anything, so a short
#          utterance is where a dictation comes back EMPTY. Nothing in
#          LibriSpeech is this short, so this has never been measured.
#   11-22  Your vocabulary. Proper nouns no recogniser has a reason to know.
#   23-30  Numbers, money, times, dates — where formatting goes wrong.
#   31-36  Spoken self-corrections. Say them exactly as written, including the
#          "no wait" — the raw transcript is supposed to contain it, and the
#          cleanup dropping it is scored separately.
#   37-44  Client and work sentences, the way you would actually dictate them.
#   45-50  Longer prose, for the tail of the length distribution.

# --- 1-10: short utterances (the empty-transcript risk case) ---
On my way.
Sounds good.

Yes, that works.
Send it through.
Not today.
I will check tonight.
Thanks, got it.
Can you call me?

# --- 31-36: spoken self-corrections (say them exactly, including the cue) ---
Send it to Noah, no wait, send it to Carlo.
Let us meet at three, actually make that four.
The invoice is for five hundred, sorry, fifteen hundred dollars.
I was going to the, I mean, I went to the shop.
We should, we should probably ship it tomorrow.
Book him in for Tuesday, no, Wednesday morning.

# --- 37-44: client and work sentences ---
I think the hero section needs more contrast and a bit more breathing room.
The client wants the booking form above the fold on mobile.
Could you double check the pricing table before I send this over?
Let me know if Tuesday afternoon works better for the call.
Make sure the contact form actually sends before we call it finished.
The dark mode version needs slightly warmer greys than the light one.
I have organised the files by client rather than by date.
The colour on the second panel does not quite match the brand.

# --- 45-50: longer prose ---
I spent most of yesterday debugging a permissions issue that turned out to be a typo.
There is no point optimising something that nobody has actually complained about.
It turns out the bug only happens when the window is resized quickly, which took a while to work out.
I would rather do it properly once than rush it and end up redoing the whole thing twice.
If the numbers do not move after a week, we will try something else entirely rather than keep tuning it.
Honestly, the second draft reads much better than the first one did, so I am going to keep going in that direction.
