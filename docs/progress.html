Where it actually stands Flow is ahead on raw accuracy by three words — 27 errors against 24 on the same 1,138. That gap is not statistically meaningful, and it is not a win either. Quill wins latency by more than two orders of magnitude, wins offline outright, and matches on formatter fidelity. The honest one-liner is: level on recognition, far ahead on everything around it.

Log

day 1 Apple's contextual-string biasing does nothing. Same audio, 0 vs 25 terms, byte-identical output.
day 1 Flow baseline banked: 2.11% WER over 50 clips, 0 failures.
day 2 Live text shipped. The recogniser returns the whole sentence every update and revises it, so it is a diff, not an append.
day 2 The latency card was lying. 12.11s shown as "key release to text" was key-down to text — it contained the whole utterance. The real figure is 3ms.
day 2 audioDurationMs was nil on all 230 records. Both transcribers wrote it into a timeline nobody read; two Insights cards had been dead since the day they were built.
day 3 The eval harness was eating its own corpus. A child process read the manifest off stdin, so clip two arrived as "089-134686-0002" — leading 1 gone — and every clip after the first was corrupted or skipped. A 50-clip run silently became 25 and still printed "ok" for each.
day 3 Fixed cause and class: manifest now read on fd 3, every child denied stdin, and the run refuses to report at all if it saw fewer clips than the manifest holds.
day 3 Fixed settle → poll. Six clips in thirty-five were called failures while their transcripts sat in history, written a moment after the reader gave up.
day 3 Tuning our own recogniser needs no microphone at all. WER is scored on raw ASR, so a file-fed harness A/Bs two configs in 60 seconds instead of 35 minutes.
day 3 Reversed the accuracy win. Better on files, lost a quarter of dictations through a microphone. Shipped config unchanged; both numbers reported.
day 3 Live text proven live — the target document sampled mid-speech, growing at t=5s and t=7s, with the final pass adding the commas the live stream lacked.
day 3 An empty transcript is no longer silent. It used to hide the overlay and return, which is indistinguishable from a dead mic, a dead key and a dead app.
day 3 Transforms and Help built. No section is a placeholder now except Notetaker, which still refuses rather than fakes.
day 3 Unfound: a residual 8-15%% of clips fail to read back through run_eval. Every silent path in the app was instrumented and stayed quiet while a dictation completed each time, so it is the rig — but unfound is unfound.
