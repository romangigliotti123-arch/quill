Waiting on you — 5 things only you can do Everything else is automated. These genuinely can't be done from here, and the rig can't produce an honest number until 1–3 are done.
  1. brew install --cask blackhole-2ch — needs your admin password, then forces one reboot. It's the virtual device that feeds both apps identical audio.
  2. Sign into Wispr Flow. It's cloud-only and does nothing without an account; free tier's 2,000 words/week covers the eval.
  3. In Flow's settings: Microphone → Auto-detect, and rebind push-to-talk off Fn to something like Ctrl+Shift+D. Synthesised Fn is ignored by macOS (Apple bug FB9093710), so the rig physically cannot start a Flow dictation until that changes.
  4. Tick Quill in Privacy & Security → Accessibility + Microphone, once it's launchable.
  5. Later: ~15 minutes reading a script into rig/record_voice.sh, so the numbers are about your voice and not a stranger's.
